The quick answer

What is the sheet actually for?

A JoyaGoo spreadsheet is a reference list for organizing product links and comparing information before ordering through a shopping agent. Treat each row as a starting point: confirm the source, variant, measurements and current costs on the destination listing.

This beginner spreadsheet guide focuses on the checks behind a useful shortlist. Shopping links on this page lead to CAFANS, not directly to JoyaGoo.

02 / A practical method

Three passes.
One clearer decision.

This JoyaGoo shopping guide begins before checkout. Record what you know, identify what is missing and resolve the gaps before committing.

Save the exact source.

For JoyaGoo product links, record the source product ID and the date checked. Finish listing variant selection before comparing: a different color, material or size may change the offer.

Compare the evidence.

Distinguish seller listing photos from warehouse inspection images. For JoyaGoo QC photos, match the order and variant, then examine labels, dimensions and visible condition.

Check the whole order.

Use a final order checklist: item, size, color, costs, delivery address and return eligibility window. Save the order reference for later parcel tracking updates.

03 / The measurement desk

Compare numbers,
not size letters.

Build your own JoyaGoo size guide around an item that already fits. Compare like-for-like measurements and keep units consistent.

What to check before saving a find
Category Useful comparison Resolve before ordering
Shoes Use sneaker size conversion as a starting point, then check shoe insole measurements for the specific model. Confirm the listed size system and whether a measurement describes the foot, insole or outsole.
Hoodies & sweaters Compare hoodie chest measurements, body length and sleeve length with a garment laid flat. Check whether the chart gives a flat chest width or a full chest circumference.
Jackets & sets Compare jacket shoulder width and room for your usual layers. Measure both pieces of a set separately. Ask how dropped shoulders or raglan sleeves are measured; a single shoulder number can mislead.
Pants & shorts Check pants inseam length, waist and rise against a pair you wear comfortably. Confirm whether an elastic waist is measured relaxed or stretched.
T-shirts & jerseys Use a clothing measurement chart for chest, length and sleeves; compare the intended silhouette. Clarify the measurement method and any stated manufacturing tolerance.
Underwear Compare the waistband range and fabric stretch using your own measurements. Check hygiene-related return restrictions before opening or trying on an item.

The number beside the item is only the start.

  • For CNY to USD conversion, record the rate used and check the amount actually charged by your payment provider.
  • An international parcel estimate depends on the destination, service and billable weight. Ask how volumetric shipping weight is calculated.
  • Check domestic delivery charges and payment processing costs separately; neither should be assumed to be included.

04 / Direct answers

Before you
open a listing.

Short answers to the questions that matter when comparing links, photos, fit and costs.

What is a JoyaGoo spreadsheet?

A JoyaGoo spreadsheet is a reference list used to organize product links and compare details before ordering through a shopping agent. A useful entry records the source listing, chosen variant, measurements and date checked. This page explains the workflow and links to CAFANS categories; it does not provide a live spreadsheet.

Where do this page's shopping links go?

Category links open the named category on cafans.com. All other shopping links open www.cafans.com. They do not open a JoyaGoo cart, and a pictured item is not a promise of a matching CAFANS listing.

Are seller photos the same as JoyaGoo QC photos?

No. Seller listing photos illustrate a listing; warehouse inspection images should document the item received for your order. Check that any QC images match your order, size and color. Photos alone cannot establish authenticity, exact materials or long-term durability.

How should I compare sizes across listings?

Use a clothing measurement chart or model-specific shoe measurements, then compare them with an item that already fits you. Match the measurement method and units. Size letters and regional shoe numbers alone are not enough, and missing measurements should be clarified before ordering.

Does a spreadsheet price include international shipping?

Do not assume it does. Check the item price, domestic delivery charges, service and payment costs, international shipping and any applicable import charges separately. Confirm current amounts with the seller, agent and carrier before payment.

What should I do when a product link stops working?

For product link troubleshooting, compare the source product ID with your saved entry and check whether the listing or variant has changed. Do not assume a similar photo identifies the same item. If the listing is unavailable or the destination looks wrong, pause and verify before paying.

Is this an official JoyaGoo or brand website?

No. This is an independent informational page with outbound links to CAFANS. It is not operated by or affiliated with JoyaGoo, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès. Brand images are visual references, not authentication evidence, stock confirmation or endorsements.
